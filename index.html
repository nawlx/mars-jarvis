Phone actions

Jarvis can open apps, call, text, search music and start navigation. Android asks you to tap once to confirm calls and texts, and sometimes app launches. Imported contacts are saved in your Jarvis data in your own Drive.

Install on your phone

Install Jarvis to get a home-screen icon, a full-screen window with no browser bars, and offline start-up. If the button is missing, use Chrome's ⋮ menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Always listening

Phones pause the microphone when the screen is off or the browser is in the background, so Jarvis listens while the app is open. Standby mode keeps the screen on with a dark display, ideal on a stand or charger.
